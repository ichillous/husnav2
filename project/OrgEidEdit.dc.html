Eid is often prayed somewhere larger than the masjid: a hall, a field, a stadium. Set the place, the prayer times, what people should know and where to park. This one page carries all of it to your profile, your TV displays and the people who follow you.

Confirmed: Eid is Tuesday, March 9. Your profile, your TV displays and everyone who follows you were told just now.

Until you confirm, people see both possible days. Confirming takes one press, the evening before.
