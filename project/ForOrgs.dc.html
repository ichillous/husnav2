What you can do

Everything your community
asks for, in one place.

Events

Post once. Seen everywhere.

An event you publish appears on your profile and in Columbus discovery in the same moment. RSVPs tell you how many chairs to set out. Events are always free to list and free to attend.

Friday prayers

Every Friday, current.

Times, khateebs, topics and languages for each service, updated in under a minute. Copy last week and change only what moved.

Prayer times and TV displays

Your times, on the wall.

Set your iqamah once and it appears on your profile, in members’ weeks and on any screen in the building. The same screen shows the Friday schedule, events and a giving code, and goes quiet during the prayer.

Programs and weekend school

Enrollment without paper forms.

Parents add their children once, choose classes, sign your policies and pay online. You review applications, place students, keep rosters and take attendance from a phone.

Giving

Donations with no platform cut.

One-time and monthly gifts to the funds you define, paid out to your bank account. Husna takes 0% of donations.

Announcements

One message, the right people.

Post to your public profile, to your followers, or only to the families in one class. Email goes out with it.

Team

Share the work safely.

Give the events volunteer, the treasurer and each teacher exactly the access they need. Teachers see only their own class.

When money moves

$0 to Husna

Donations and tuition go to your bank account through Stripe. Stripe charges its own card processing on each payment, and that goes to Stripe. Husna adds nothing and keeps nothing.

You connect your bank once during setup.

How Husna is paid for

By people who choose to. Husna is built and looked after by one developer in Columbus, and anyone who finds it useful can buy the developer a coffee. Nothing changes if you never do.

Why we verify

Families trust what they find here.

Only verified organizations can publish events, collect enrollments or receive donations. Verification means a real person checked that the applicant represents the organization, using an organization email address, a document or a phone call to its public number.

Before you apply

Fair questions.

Who should apply?

Someone who can speak for the organization: an imam, a board member, an administrator or a school director. You can invite the rest of the team afterwards.

We already have a website. Is this extra work?

Keep your website. Husna is where families already look for what is on this week, and it replaces paper enrollment forms and group-chat schedules.

What do parents need to enroll a child?

A free Husna account. They add each child once and reuse those details for every class and every term.

Who sees children’s information?

The family, and the staff and teachers you give access to. Never the public, and never other organizations.

We are not in Columbus. Can we join?

Husna is open in Columbus first. Apply anyway and we will tell you when your city opens.

Can we leave?

Yes. You can export your rosters and donation records and close the workspace at any time.
