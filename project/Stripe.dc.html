Hand-offs

Where Husna hands over to Stripe.

Four moments leave Husna for a page Stripe hosts. Each one returns to a Husna screen. The buttons below continue the prototype as if the person had finished on Stripe.

1 · Checkout

Paying a donation or a payment link

Opens from the Donate page and from an organization’s payment link.

Stripe’s own page appears hereHosted and designed by Stripe. Husna does not draw or control it, and never sees card or bank numbers.

What Husna sends

  • Amount and currency, for example $50.00
  • What it is for: “Donation to Crescent House Masjid · General fund”
  • One time or monthly
  • The email for the receipt, when the person is signed in
  • Statement text: [STATEMENT DESCRIPTOR]
  • Husna’s share: none. The whole amount is for the organization

What comes back

  • Paid: Husna shows the receipt and emails it
  • Cancelled: back to the form, nothing charged
  • Declined: Stripe explains why and the person can try another card there
Donation paid →Donor cancelsBack to the payment link

2 · Saving a payment method

Adding or replacing a card or bank account

Opens from step 5 of enrollment, from Payments, and from a waitlist offer. Nothing is charged here.

Stripe’s own page appears hereHosted and designed by Stripe. Husna does not draw or control it, and never sees card or bank numbers.

What Husna sends

  • The signed-in member’s Stripe customer record
  • Card and US bank account as allowed methods
  • Where to return: the screen the person came from

What comes back

  • Saved: Husna shows “Visa ending 4242” and uses it for the next scheduled payment
  • Cancelled: the previous method stays in place
  • A failed scheduled payment can be retried with the new method straight away
Method saved, back to Payments →Back to enrollment, step 5Back to the waitlist offer

3 · Connecting payouts

An organization connects its bank account

Opens from the setup guide and from Tuition and payouts. Only an owner can start it.

Stripe’s own page appears hereHosted and designed by Stripe. Husna does not draw or control it, and never sees card or bank numbers.

What Husna sends

  • Organization legal name, address and tax status from Settings
  • The owner’s name and email
  • Where to return: Tuition and payouts

What comes back

  • Active: donations and tuition can be taken, payouts follow Stripe’s schedule
  • More information needed: Husna shows what Stripe asked for and by when
  • Not finished: the Connect button stays, and the giving page and paid enrollment stay off
Payouts connected →Owner leaves before finishing

4 · A coffee for the developer

A member or an organization buys the developer a coffee

Opens from the coffee button: in the footer and account for members, in the sidebar and settings for organizations. It is the only payment that goes to Husna.

Stripe’s own page appears hereHosted and designed by Stripe. Husna does not draw or control it, and never sees card or bank numbers.

What Husna sends

  • The amount, one time, for example $5.00
  • What it is for: “A coffee for Husna’s developer”
  • The email for the receipt, when the person is signed in
  • Paid to Husna’s own Stripe account, never an organization’s

What comes back

  • Paid: Husna says thank you and emails the receipt
  • Cancelled: back to the sheet, nothing charged
  • Declined: Stripe explains why and the person can try another card there
Back to a member’s coffeeBack to an organization’s coffee
