How it works

  1. On the TVOpen its browser and go to husna.app/tv. It shows a code.
  2. Here, on a computer or phonePress Pair a TV and type that code.
  3. Choose what it showsPrayer times, the Friday schedule, announcements, school events and slides.
  4. SaveThe TV picks up every change within a minute. Your computer or phone can be closed; the TV carries on by itself.

Any TV or monitor with a web browser works, or a streaming stick or small computer plugged into one. Tested on: [TESTED TV DEVICES]. If the connection drops, the screen keeps showing prayer times for 30 days.
