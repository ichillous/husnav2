Members and parents

Do I need an account?

No account is needed to browse events and organizations or to donate. You need one to RSVP, save events, follow organizations and enroll children.

Is anything on Husna paid?

Husna itself is free, for everyone. Events are always free to attend. Some classes charge tuition, which is set by the organization, shown before you enroll, and goes to the organization.

What is a home masjid?

The masjid you pray at most. Choose it from the masjid’s page and its adhan and iqamah times stay at the top of every page you open. Change or remove it in Account.

How do I enroll my child in a class?

Open the program page, choose Enroll a child, and follow the five steps. You can save and return at any point.

When am I charged tuition?

Only after the school confirms your child’s place. Waitlisted children are never charged.

How do I change or cancel an RSVP?

Open the event from Saved, then choose Change RSVP or Cancel RSVP. The host’s count updates at once.

I cannot sign in.

Use Email me a link on the sign-in page, or reset your password.

Organizations

How do we join?

Apply from For organizations. We verify every application before a profile goes live.

What does Husna charge?

Nothing. Profiles, events, classes, tuition, donations, payment links and TV displays are all free, with no plan to upgrade to. When someone pays by card, Stripe charges its own processing; Husna adds nothing to it.

Then how is Husna paid for?

By people who choose to. Husna is built by one developer, and members and organizations who find it useful can buy the developer a coffee. It is a gift, never a condition.

Can we show prayer times on a TV?

Yes. Open husna.app/tv in the browser of any TV, streaming stick or small computer, then type the code it shows under TV displays in your workspace. It is free.

How do payouts work?

You connect a bank account through Stripe during setup. Tuition and donations are paid out on Stripe’s schedule.

Someone listed our organization without permission.

Tell us using the form below and choose Report a listing. We will suspend the profile while we check.
