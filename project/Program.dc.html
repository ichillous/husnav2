Pay in full, or half now and half on November 24. You are charged only after the school confirms your child’s place.

04 · How enrollment works

About ten minutes, from your phone.

  1. 1Create a Husna account. Free, with your email. One account covers the whole family.
  2. 2Add your children. Name, date of birth and anything the teacher should know.
  3. 3Choose a class for each child. If a class is full you can join its waitlist.
  4. 4Confirm family and safety details. Guardians, an emergency contact and who may pick up.
  5. 5Sign the agreements and choose how to pay. The school reviews applications within three days and emails you the result.

Have these ready

  • Each child’s date of birth
  • An emergency contact who is not a parent
  • Names of everyone allowed to pick up
  • Allergies or medical notes, if any
  • A card or bank account for tuition

05 · Questions parents ask

Good to know.

My child has never read Arabic. Which level?

Choose the level for their age. The teacher checks reading on the first weekend and places beginners in a starter group within the class.

What happens if a class is full?

Join the waitlist at no cost. If a place opens you get an email and 48 hours to accept it before it passes to the next family.

Can we join after the term starts?

Yes, until November 8 where a class has room. Tuition is reduced for the weekends already past.

How do I report an absence?

From your child’s page on Husna, any time before 9:00 AM that day. The teacher sees it on the attendance sheet.

Who can see my child’s information?

You, the guardians you add, and the school staff and teacher for the class. It is never shown publicly.

Who do I ask about something else?

The school office answers messages on weekdays. Message Crescent House Masjid
