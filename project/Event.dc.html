Family · Saturday, October 17, 2026

Weekend Qur’an School Open House

Meet the teachers, walk through the classrooms and see how a weekend morning runs. Bring your children, and bring your questions about levels, curriculum and enrollment.

Families who are ready can enroll on the spot. A volunteer will help you add your children and sign the forms on your phone.

What to expect

  • 9:30 AMWelcome, tea and a short introduction to the school
  • 10:00 AMClassroom visits with each of the four teachers
  • 10:45 AMQuestions with the school director, Ustadha Noor Hassan
  • 11:15 AMHelp with enrollment for families who want it
