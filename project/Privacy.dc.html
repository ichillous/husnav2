In plain words

Children’s information

  • Children never have accounts and never appear on public pages or in search.
  • A child’s record is created by a parent or guardian and is visible only to that family.
  • A school sees a child’s record only after the parent enrolls the child there, and only its staff and that child’s teacher.
  • Husna staff cannot open children’s names, health notes or school records.
  • A parent can correct or delete a child’s record at any time from My family.
