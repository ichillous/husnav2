Eid al-Fitr 1448 · Crescent House Masjid

Eid prayer, together.

Day confirmedTuesday, March 9, 2027. Confirmed on Monday evening, after the moon was sighted.
Day not yet confirmedTuesday, March 9 or Wednesday, March 10, 2027. The masjid confirms the evening before, and this page changes at once.
Remind me the evening before
This prayer is not at the masjid. It is at Riverbend Fairgrounds, about 15 minutes from Crescent House by car. The masjid itself is closed on Eid morning.

Prayer times

1stTakbir from7:00 AMPrayer7:30 AMImam Adam RahmanKhutbah in English, after the prayer
2ndTakbir from9:00 AMPrayer9:30 AMShaykh Yusuf AliKhutbah in Arabic and English, after the prayer

Two prayers so that everyone has room. Come to either. Doors open at 6:30 AM.

Share a ride if you can. Volunteers direct cars from 6:15 AM. Once you are through the gate, please follow them and not your maps app.

Good to know

  • Come with wudu and bring a prayer mat; the hall floor is bare.
  • The khutbah follows the prayer.
  • A festival for children follows the second prayer, 10:30 AM to 1:00 PM, free.

Before the prayer

Zakat al-Fitr

Crescent House asks that it be given before the Eid prayer: $12.00 for each person in your household.
